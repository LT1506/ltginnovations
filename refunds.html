Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

This policy explains how billing, cancellations, and refunds work for the Chairtime platform provided by LTG Innovations LLC.

Setup fee

The one-time setup fee covers onboarding and configuration of your account. Because this work is performed up front, the setup fee is generally non-refundable once onboarding has begun.

Monthly subscription

The Chairtime subscription is billed monthly in advance. You may cancel at any time; your subscription remains active through the end of the current billing period, and you will not be charged for the following period. Monthly fees already paid are non-refundable except where required by law.

How to cancel

To cancel your subscription, contact us at lgrayson990@gmail.com. We will confirm your cancellation and the date your access ends.

Payments to stylists

In-person card payments a client makes to a stylist through Chairtime are transactions between that client and the stylist. Any refund of a service payment is handled by the stylist through their own payment account. LTG Innovations is the software provider and is not a party to those service transactions.

Questions

If you have a billing question, email lgrayson990@gmail.com and we'll help.

This document is a plain-language summary and is not legal advice. Specific terms agreed to at sign-up will govern your subscription.
