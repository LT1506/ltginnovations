KPI Studio

Monthly KPI reporting automation for Progressive Health. Internship, 2026.

A ~5,900-line Python desktop app that replaces a manual monthly process. It extracts numbers from Excel workbooks, matches them against leadership's answer keys, flags anything that needs a human look, and writes results back. Validated at 91 to 96% accuracy across five months. I also built an offline RTM billing tool that turns billing exports into per-patient cards for clinicians.

Python, Excel automation, desktop GUI

Internal tool, not public

RangeHUD

Rifle ballistics calculator for smart glasses. Personal project, 2026.

Build a rifle profile, enter wind, incline, and conditions, and read your holds on a Meta Ray-Ban Display. Velocity truing, imperial and metric units, CSV export, and full offline support as an installable web app.

JavaScript, HTML and CSS, PWA

TrailCompass

Live hiking compass for smart glasses. Personal project, 2026.

A real-time compass driven by device sensors and GPS. Drop waypoints, get a bearing and distance back to any of them, check sunset and daylight offline, and drive the whole app with the glasses' gesture band. Heading accuracy verified on the glasses.

JavaScript, Geolocation, device sensors, PWA
