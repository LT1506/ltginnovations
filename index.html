What Chairtime is

One tool to run the day — take bookings, send reminders, and get paid at the chair.

Chairtime replaces the patchwork of paper calendars, text-message bookings, and separate card readers with a single, mobile-first platform. Stylists manage their schedule, their clients, and their in-person payments in one place — while LTG Innovations handles the software, hosting, and updates.

Who it's for

Independent stylists

Run your own book, your own hours, your own payments — without juggling five apps.

Booth & chair renters

Keep your clients and revenue clearly your own, with payments that go straight to you.

Small salons

Add each stylist as they come aboard and keep every calendar in one clean place.

Features

Everything the day needs, nothing it doesn't.

Online booking & calendar

A clean calendar with day, week, and month views. Book, reschedule, and see the whole day at a glance.

In-person card payments

Tap-and-go card payments at the chair with a Stripe reader — money lands in the stylist's own account.

Automated reminders

Appointment confirmations and reminders go out on their own, cutting no-shows without lifting a finger.

Client management

Each stylist keeps their own private client list — contact details, history, and notes, scoped to them alone.

Revenue dashboard

See earnings by day, week, or month — a simple, honest picture of how the business is doing.

Works on any device

Phone, tablet, or computer — Chairtime is mobile-first and installs to the home screen like an app.
