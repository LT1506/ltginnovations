Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LTG Innovations LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our Services, including the Chairtime platform.

Information we collect

  • Account information — the name, email address, phone number, and business details a stylist or salon provides when signing up.
  • Client and booking information — the appointment details and client contact information that Customers enter to run their business.
  • Usage information — basic technical data (such as device and log information) needed to operate and secure the Services.

How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Services.
  • To send booking confirmations, reminders, and service-related messages.
  • To provide support and to keep the Services secure.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Payment information

Card payments are processed by Stripe. Payment card details are handled directly by Stripe under its own privacy and security practices; LTG Innovations does not store full card numbers. Please review Stripe's Privacy Policy for details on how payment data is handled.

How information is shared

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us operate the Services (such as our payment processor and hosting provider), and when required by law.

Data retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the Services and to meet our legal obligations. Customers may request deletion of their account data as described below.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us. Customers control the client information they enter and are responsible for handling it appropriately.

Contact

For any privacy question or request, email hello@ltginnovations.com.
